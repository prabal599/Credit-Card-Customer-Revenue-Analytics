# 💳 Credit Card Customer & Revenue Analytics

> **End-to-End Data Analytics Project | Python • SQL • Power BI • Excel**

An end-to-end data analytics project focused on analyzing **credit card customers, transactions, revenue, customer behavior, and risk indicators**.

The project transforms raw customer and transaction data into actionable business insights using **Python for data cleaning and analysis, SQL for business queries, and Power BI for interactive dashboards**.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Financial institutions generate large amounts of customer and transaction data. Analyzing this data can help identify:

* High-value customers
* Revenue-generating customer segments
* Customer spending patterns
* Declining or inactive customers
* Risky customer segments
* Payment behavior
* Popular merchant categories
* Credit utilization patterns
* Opportunities to increase card usage and revenue

This project demonstrates how a Data Analyst can convert raw financial data into **business-focused insights and recommendations**.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

1. Identify the customers generating the highest revenue.
2. Analyze customer spending and transaction behavior.
3. Understand revenue distribution across cities and customer segments.
4. Identify inactive or potentially lost customers.
5. Analyze payment status and credit risk indicators.
6. Understand which merchant categories generate the most transactions and revenue.
7. Analyze credit limits and outstanding balances.
8. Identify opportunities to increase customer engagement and card usage.
9. Build an interactive Power BI dashboard for business decision-making.
